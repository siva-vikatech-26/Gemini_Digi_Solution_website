FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Gemini Digital Solution do?

We are an AI product development company with offices in Trichy and Chennai. We design and build AI-powered CRMs, web applications, chatbots and AI agents, workflow automation, machine learning models and data pipelines. One partner from idea to launch, and beyond.

Can you add AI to the software we already use?

Yes. Whether you need a product from scratch or AI added to the tools you already use, we scope it, build it and keep it running. We connect your existing systems through APIs and automation tools such as n8n.

How long does an AI project take?

It depends on the scope. After a free consultation you get a clear scope and estimate. We then work in short sprints with a working demo every week or two, so you see progress and can steer early.

Do your chatbots and CRMs support Tamil and WhatsApp?

Yes. We build WhatsApp-first workflows, and our CRMs can record calls and produce transcripts and summaries in Tamil and English. Chatbots and AI agents are trained on your own data.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. Your code, your data and your models belong to you, with clear documentation and a full handover whenever you want it.

What happens after launch?

We deploy, train your team and keep improving the system with monitoring, updates and support plans.
